計算原理 · Calculation Principle

中文說明

降雨會大量稀釋養殖池的鹽度，同時因雨水偏酸而降低pH 值。 健康海水本身具有緩衝能力，能抵抗 pH 驟降，但緩衝容量消耗後將造成魚蝦急性壓力或死亡。

本計算以鹽度稀釋比例推算池中雨水佔比， 再比較「若無緩衝力」理論 pH 與實測池水 pH 的差距， 反推目前的剩餘緩衝容量。

English

Heavy rain dilutes salinity and lowers pH due to the acidity of rainwater. Healthy seawater has a natural buffering capacity that resists pH drops, but once depleted, livestock experience acute stress or mortality.

This tool uses salinity dilution to estimate the rain fraction in the pond, then compares the theoretical "no-buffer" pH with the actual measured pH to back-calculate remaining buffer capacity.

① Rain fraction 雨水佔比
rain% = (S_inlet − S_pool) / (S_inlet − S_rain)
② Estimated pH (no buffering) 無緩衝 pH 估計
pH_est = −log₁₀( [H⁺]_inlet × (1−rain%) + [H⁺]_rain × rain% )
③ Buffer Capacity 緩衝容量
Buffer% = ( [H⁺]_est − [H⁺]_pool ) / ( [H⁺]_rain × rain% ) × 100%
