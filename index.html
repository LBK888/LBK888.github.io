Research研究方向

Segmentation clock體節時鐘

Delta–Notch signalling · zebrafish embryosDelta–Notch 訊號 · 斑馬魚胚胎

A vertebrate body is laid down in segments, one at a time, by a genetic oscillator in the embryo's tail. Raising Delta–Notch signalling speeds that clock up and gives zebrafish more, shorter segments; when the rhythm is disrupted, the pattern repairs itself as neighbouring cells resynchronise and the tissue flows.脊椎動物的身體由體節一節一節排出來，背後是胚胎尾端的基因振盪器。提高 Delta–Notch 訊號會讓這個時鐘變快，斑馬魚因而長出更多、更短的體節；節奏被打亂時，相鄰細胞重新同步、組織持續流動，圖樣便會自我修復。

  • Nature Communications 2016
  • eLife 2021
  • Molecular Neurobiology 2024

Tissue patterning組織圖樣

Spatial statistics · lateral inhibition空間統計 · 側向抑制

Is a scatter of cells clustered, random or evenly spaced? In zebrafish skin, ionocyte progenitors are spaced apart by Delta–Notch lateral inhibition. We build the tools that answer the question with Ripley's K and L(r), and generate reference patterns to test against.一群細胞是聚集、隨機，還是均勻分布？斑馬魚皮膚中的離子細胞前驅細胞，靠 Delta–Notch 側向抑制彼此拉開距離。我們開發以 Ripley's K 與 L(r) 回答這個問題的工具，也產生可供比對的參考圖樣。

Cell fate細胞命運

Cellular automata · cell shape models細胞自動機 · 細胞形狀模型

Simple rules between neighbours can decide what each cell becomes. Our multi-species cellular automata let students write those rules and pit them against each other to see which patterns survive; Voronoi Cell ODE couples cell shapes to small gene circuits.相鄰細胞之間的簡單規則，就能決定每顆細胞的命運。多物種細胞自動機讓學生自己寫規則、彼此對戰，看哪種圖樣能存活；Voronoi Cell ODE 則把細胞形狀與小型基因迴路連在一起。

Machine vision機器視覺

YOLO models · zebrafish larvaeYOLO 模型 · 斑馬魚幼魚

We train detection models on our own zebrafish images: zVision for the fish room, TrackBox for imaging six- and seven-day-old larvae, and browser tools that read a heart rate from a video. The models are exported to run on a phone, with no server.我們用自己的斑馬魚影像訓練偵測模型：zVision 用於魚房，TrackBox 用於拍攝 6～7 天大的幼魚，還有能從影片讀出心率的網頁工具。模型都匯出成可在手機上直接執行的格式，不需要伺服器。

Aquaculture data養殖數據

Sensors · OCR · forecasting感測器 · OCR · 預測

Ponds need watching. We read meters that only have a screen, stream ESP32 water sensors, forecast with Kalman filters, Holt smoothing and recurrent networks, and estimate how much rain a mariculture pond can take before a storm. Earlier field work measured greenhouse gases from shrimp ponds.養殖池需要時時看顧。我們讀取只有螢幕的儀表、串接 ESP32 水質感測器，以卡爾曼濾波、Holt 平滑與遞迴神經網路做預測，也估算海水養殖池在暴雨前還能承受多少雨量。先前的野外研究則量測了白蝦養殖池的溫室氣體。
